    Search for an address or click on the map. The report brings together what national open datasets record about that spot and its surroundings, decade by decade.

    It is written to support conversations about past land use, for example before establishing a garden, buying land or planning soil tests. A past activity on the Hazardous Activities and Industries List does not mean land is contaminated, and contamination can occur where nothing is recorded.
